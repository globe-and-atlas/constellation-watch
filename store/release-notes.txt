Initial release of Constellation for Pebble Time 2 (Emery).
- 3D Celestial Orbit Cage visualizing global GNSS constellation planes (GPS, Galileo, GLONASS, BeiDou).
- Polar Skyplot with azimuth/elevation bearing and live satellite radar.
- Geodesy & DOP Matrix with real-time 4x4 Gauss-Jordan geometry matrix inversion (PDOP, HDOP, VDOP, TDOP, GDOP).
- 2D Equirectangular Ground Track projection with observer footprint.
- Real-time SGP4 propagation and CelesTrak ephemeris updates via PebbleKit JS.
